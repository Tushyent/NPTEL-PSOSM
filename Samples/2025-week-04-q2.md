## Question

What are the different forms of Privacy?

## Options

- **A.** Information
- **B.** Communication
- **C.** Territorial
- **D.** Bodily

## Accepted answer

**A. Information; B. Communication; C. Territorial; D. Bodily**

## Explanation

The lecture lists four forms of privacy: information privacy (data about a person), communication privacy (messages and calls), territorial privacy (home, city and country) and bodily privacy (the person's physical self). All four options are correct.
