## Question

Which of the following is/are e-crimes on online social media ?

## Options

- **A.** Phishing
- **B.** Clickbaiting
- **C.** Work From Home Scam
- **D.** All of the Above

## Accepted answer

**D. All of the Above**

## Explanation

Phishing, clickbaiting and work-from-home scams are all discussed as eCrimes on online social media. Since each of the first three options is an example, the accepted answer is "All of the Above".
