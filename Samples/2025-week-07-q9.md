## Question

Denial of Service Attacks and Buffer Overflow Attacks fall under :

## Options

- **A.** Physical Attacks
- **B.** Semantic Attacks
- **C.** Syntactic Attacks
- **D.** None of the above

## Accepted answer

**C. Syntactic Attacks**

## Explanation

Syntactic attacks target programs and systems - for example denial-of-service and buffer-overflow attacks. Semantic attacks target meaning and human trust, such as phishing or disinformation. These two attacks therefore belong to the syntactic class.
