## Question

“Please Rob Me” highlighted issues specifically stemming from:

## Options

- **A.** Public Self-Disclosure of Information on Social Media by users
- **B.** Data leaks from social media websites due to hacking attempts
- **C.** Sharing passwords with your friends on Social Media
- **D.** None of the Above

## Accepted answer

**A. Public Self-Disclosure of Information on Social Media by users**

## Explanation

"Please Rob Me" collected public location-sharing posts and showed when people were likely away from home. The weakness was public self-disclosure by users, not a server hack, password sharing or an unknown category.

## Source citation

- **Topic:** Week 4.1: Public self-disclosure and Please Rob Me
- **Page:** Pages 325-327
- **Summary:** Please Rob Me used public tweets about being away from home to show that a location post can reveal an empty house.
