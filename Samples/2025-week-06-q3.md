## Question

A group of hackers decide to target the Managing Director of a Company with the intention of stealing his/her credit card details by sending suspicious emails. As discussed in the lecture,this is called ______ .

## Options

- **A.** Clickbaiting
- **B.** Whaling
- **C.** Account Compromise
- **D.** None of the above

## Accepted answer

**B. Whaling**

## Explanation

Whaling is a targeted phishing attack aimed at senior executives such as a managing director or CEO. Vishing uses phone calls, smishing uses SMS, and clickbaiting is a misleading-link technique rather than the executive-targeted attack described here.
