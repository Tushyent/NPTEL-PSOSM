## Question

What is the primary goal of k-anonymity in data privacy?

## Options

- **A.** Ensure that every attribute in the dataset is unique.
- **B.** Prevent unauthorised access to the dataset.
- **C.** Minimize the amount of data collected.
- **D.** Making it difficult to link specific individuals to their records.

## Accepted answer

**D. Making it difficult to link specific individuals to their records.**

## Explanation

k-anonymity reduces re-identification risk by making each record indistinguishable from at least k-1 other records on the quasi-identifiers. The practical goal is to make it difficult to link a specific person back to their record. It does not promise unique attributes, access control or data minimisation.
