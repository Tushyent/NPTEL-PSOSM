## Question

Which of the following is/are shortcomings of online social media platforms?

## Options

- **A.** Spread of misinformation
- **B.** Cyberbullying and harassment
- **C.** Addiction and time consumption
- **D.** limited control over privacy

## Accepted answer

**A. Spread of misinformation; B. Cyberbullying and harassment; C. Addiction and time consumption; D. limited control over privacy**

## Explanation

All four are widely documented problems with online social media. Platforms make it easy to spread misinformation, host harassment and cyberbullying, encourage compulsive use, and make it hard for users to control what is shared about them. The official key accepts all four statements.
