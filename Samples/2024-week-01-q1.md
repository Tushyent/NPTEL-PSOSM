## Question

1.  Which of the following is/are anonymous social networking sites ?

## Options

- **A.** Whisper
- **B.** Sarahah
- **C.** Facebook
- **D.** X (Formerly Twitter)

## Accepted answer

**A. Whisper; B. Sarahah**

## Explanation

Whisper and Sarahah are anonymous social networks: the identity of the person posting is not shown. Facebook and X (formerly Twitter) are identified networks where posts are tied to an account, so only the first two are accepted.

## Source citation

- **Topic:** Week 1.1: Anonymous social networks
- **Page:** Pages 16-17
- **Summary:** Anonymous social networks such as Whisper let people post content without revealing who they are.
