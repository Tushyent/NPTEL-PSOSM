## Question

Which of the following is/are location-based services?

## Options

- **A.** Foursquare
- **B.** Ola
- **C.** Yelp
- **D.** Uber

## Accepted answer

**A. Foursquare; B. Ola; C. Yelp; D. Uber**

## Explanation

Foursquare, Yelp, Ola and Uber all use a user's location to provide a service. They are therefore location-based services, and all four are accepted.
