## Question

Which of the following 4 V’s of Social Media addresses the correctness of information posted online ?

## Options

- **A.** Velocity
- **B.** Veracity
- **C.** Volume
- **D.** Variety

## Accepted answer

**B. Veracity**

## Explanation

Veracity is the V that addresses correctness: whether the information posted is accurate, legitimate and trustworthy. Velocity is speed of generation, volume is scale and variety is the mix of content types.
