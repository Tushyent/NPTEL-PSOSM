## Question

An indicator similar to hashtags is called ______ on Reddit.

## Options

- **A.** Flair
- **B.** Karma
- **C.** Comment
- **D.** Upvote

## Accepted answer

**A. Flair**

## Explanation

A flair is Reddit's label for a post or community. It works like a hashtag or tag by grouping posts by topic. Karma is a reputation score, while comments and upvotes are actions users take.
