## Question

How would you identify emotional and psychological states based on the written text?

## Options

- **A.** LIWC
- **B.** K-means clustering
- **C.** N-gram analysis
- **D.** All of the above

## Accepted answer

**A. LIWC**

## Explanation

LIWC (Linguistic Inquiry and Word Count) takes a body of text and reports the percentage of words that fall into psychological and linguistic categories such as positive emotion, negative emotion, anxiety and sadness. K-means, n-grams and "all of the above" are not the tool described here.
